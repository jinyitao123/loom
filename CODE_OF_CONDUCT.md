# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.

In short:

- Be respectful and constructive; assume good faith.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language — in issues,
  PRs, reviews, or any project space.
- Critique code, not people. Technical disagreement argued on evidence is
  welcome and normal.
- Maintainers may remove content or ban participants who violate these rules.

Report conduct issues through the repository's **Security tab → Advisories**
(mark it as a conduct report) or by contacting the maintainer directly via
their GitHub profile. All reports are handled confidentially.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant v2.1 is available at
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
